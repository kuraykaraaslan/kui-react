# Sample process plant (IFC)

Three federated discipline models of a small process plant, used by WidgetKit's `viewer.model`
sample (the "Process plant (3D)" card on the Plant operations board and the widget gallery):

| File | Discipline |
|---|---|
| `Plant-Structure.ifc` | Steel pipe rack, process structure, stairs, foundations, paving, bund wall |
| `Plant-Process.ifc` | Distillation column, reactor, drums, exchangers, pumps, tanks, flare, process lines |
| `Plant-Utilities.ifc` | Utility piping, cable trays, firewater ring, transformers |

Source: kui-viewer (`public/samples/plant/`, generated by `scripts/generate-plant-model.py`,
commit `cd4ae57`), the models behind its Coordination demo (https://kui-viewer.kuray.dev/review.html).
They are our own and licensed under the Apache License 2.0, like kui-viewer.

Coordinates are metres, Z up; the sample sensors in `modules/app/WidgetKit/sample/sample-data.ts`
(`sample.plant.sensors`) are placed on the equipment in these models. Copy them again from kui-viewer
when the generator changes; do not edit them by hand.
